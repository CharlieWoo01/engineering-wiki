A technology evaluation compares candidate tools or services against explicit, weighted
requirements so that choices are evidence-based and the reasoning outlives the people involved.

## Process

1. **Define the problem** and what happens if nothing changes.
2. **Agree requirements** and weight them before looking at options, to avoid bias.
3. **Shortlist options**, including "do nothing" or "build it ourselves" where relevant.
4. **Gather evidence** — documentation, hands-on trials, a [proof of concept](../proofs-of-concept/).
5. **Score and recommend**, explaining the trade-offs.
6. **Record the decision** as an [ADR](../../adr/introduction/), linking back to the evaluation.
