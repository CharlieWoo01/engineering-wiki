A proof of concept (PoC) answers a specific question — "can this work for us?" — with the least
effort possible. It is timeboxed, disposable and judged against success criteria agreed up front.

## Guidelines

- **Start with a hypothesis** and measurable success criteria.
- **Timebox it.** When time runs out, report what you have.
- **Be explicit about shortcuts.** A PoC is not production-ready; say what would need to change.
- **Record the outcome**, including failures — a disproven idea is a useful result.
- **Follow up with an ADR** if the PoC leads to a decision.
