Experiments are smaller than proofs of concept: an afternoon trying a tool, a benchmark or a
spike. They rarely warrant a full report, but the result is still worth keeping.

## How to record an experiment

Add a row to the log below. If an experiment grows, promote it to a
[proof of concept](../proofs-of-concept/) or a [technical note](../../knowledge-base/technical-notes/).

| Date         | Experiment       | Question                  | Result          | Link            |
| ------------ | ---------------- | ------------------------- | --------------- | --------------- |
| _YYYY-MM-DD_ | _What you tried_ | _What you wanted to know_ | _What happened_ | _Code or notes_ |
