Developer experience (DevEx) is how easy it is for engineers to do their best work: fast
feedback, low cognitive load and long stretches of uninterrupted focus.

## Measuring

- **DORA metrics** — deployment frequency, lead time for changes, change failure rate and time
  to restore service — measure delivery performance.
- **SPACE** reminds us that productivity has several dimensions: satisfaction, performance,
  activity, communication and efficiency.
- **Surveys** capture friction that system metrics miss. Ask regularly and act visibly.

## Common improvements

- One-command local setup and a fast inner loop (edit → see the result in seconds).
- Golden-path templates for new services.
- Fast, reliable CI with clear failure messages.
- Self-service environments and documentation that is easy to find — like this wiki.
